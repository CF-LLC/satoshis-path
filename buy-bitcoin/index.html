How to buy your first bitcoin

  1. 1

    Pick a provider

    Bitcoin-only services keep things simple. Big exchanges list many coins and more features.

  2. 2

    Verify your identity

    US providers are required to check ID (KYC) before you can buy.

  3. 3

    Fund with a bank transfer

    Bank transfers usually cost less than cards. Check the provider's fee page.

  4. 4

    Start small, buy regularly

    A small recurring buy is an easy way to start without guessing the price.

  5. 5

    Move it to your own wallet

    Once you hold a meaningful amount, withdraw to a wallet only you control.
