Why this exists

Most beginners either track nothing or try to force Bitcoin into a generic budget spreadsheet. The result: they do not know their real average cost, they double-count fees, they forget which wallet holds what, and they abandon ambitious plans. This toolkit is built for ordinary budgets and the actual questions people ask after a few purchases.

What is inside (9 tabs)

01 Dashboard

Live totals: BTC purchased, cash spent, average cost per BTC, rewards, and combined position. Pulls automatically from the other tabs.

02 Purchases

Main log: date, BTC amount, total cash paid (including fees), optional fee visibility column, platform, notes, and cost-per-BTC formula. Clear rule to avoid double-counting fees.

03 Fee Log

Optional deep-dive on spreads, trading fees, and withdrawals. Many beginners discover this is their biggest quiet cost.

04 Rewards

Bitcoin earned (cashback, etc.) kept separate so it does not distort your average purchase cost.

05 Transfers

Moves between wallets or exchanges. Logged so they never get counted as new purchases.

06 Wallet Inventory

Where your Bitcoin lives (exchange / software / hardware), approximate amounts, recovery-phrase status checklist, and last verified date. Never stores the phrase itself.

07 Stacking Plan

One simple monthly target in dollars (or sats). Monthly review rows and reflection prompts so the plan stays realistic.

08 Glossary + Start Here

Plain-English terms and clear instructions. Yellow cells = your inputs. Black text = formulas.

Who it is for

  • People buying small amounts of Bitcoin over time
  • Anyone who wants to know their real average cost after fees
  • Beginners who have more than one wallet or exchange
  • People who prefer a simple plan they will actually keep

Compatibility

Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets (File → Import the .xlsx), LibreOffice Calc, and most apps that open modern Excel files. Formulas recalculate automatically.

What this is not

  • Not tax software or a complete accounting system
  • Not personalized investment or tax advice
  • Does not implement FIFO / LIFO / HIFO lot tracking
  • Does not replace official exchange or wallet records
  • Does not store recovery phrases or private keys (and you should never put them here)
